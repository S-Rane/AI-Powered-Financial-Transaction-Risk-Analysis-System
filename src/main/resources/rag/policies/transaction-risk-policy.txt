TITLE: High-Value Transaction Review Policy

PURPOSE:
Identify high-value transactions that may require additional review.

RULES:
1. Transactions exceeding INR 100000 should be flagged for additional risk assessment.
2. A high transaction amount alone does not prove fraudulent activity.
3. The risk engine must consider other available transaction attributes before assigning the final risk level.
4. Transactions identified as potentially suspicious may require manual review.

POLICY ID: TRP-002
TITLE: Unusual Transaction Location Policy

PURPOSE:
Identify transactions that may require additional location-based risk assessment.

RULES:
1. A transaction originating from an unusual location may require additional verification.
2. Location alone must not be treated as proof of fraud.
3. Consider transaction amount, transaction type, and other available signals alongside location.
4. If supporting information is insufficient, recommend further verification rather than asserting fraud.

POLICY ID: TRP-003
TITLE: Online Purchase Risk Policy

PURPOSE:
Provide guidance for assessing online purchase transactions.

RULES:
1. Online purchases should be assessed using the available transaction risk signals.
2. High-value online purchases may require additional assessment.
3. Do not classify a transaction as fraudulent solely because it is an online purchase.
4. Explain the specific risk indicators that support a recommendation.
